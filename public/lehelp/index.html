How it works

01. Share

Submit an honest request or offer.

02. Review

A volunteer checks submissions before publishing. Verification does not guarantee fulfillment.

03. Connect

Neighbors can offer assistance. For approved Cash App links, neighbors can contribute directly. Cash App tracks contributions; LeHelp does not independently verify those payments.

Never send money to an unverified person. Avoid sharing sensitive information publicly. No political affiliation is required. LeHelp is an independent community project, not a government agency or registered charity.
